# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We, as contributors and maintainers of the Student Pass System, pledge to make participation in our project and community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, race, religion, socio-economic status, or personal background.

We are committed to fostering an open, respectful, welcoming, and inclusive environment for all students, developers, educators, and contributors.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful and professional in all interactions.
- Showing empathy and kindness toward others.
- Accepting and providing constructive feedback gracefully.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them.
- Supporting collaboration and knowledge sharing.
- Focusing on improving the project and helping the community.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, bullying, or intimidation of any kind.
- Offensive, discriminatory, or derogatory comments.
- Personal attacks or hostile behavior.
- Sharing private information without permission.
- Disruptive behavior that negatively affects collaboration.
- Any conduct that would be considered inappropriate in a professional or educational environment.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, pull requests, issues, or other contributions that violate these guidelines. When appropriate, maintainers will explain moderation decisions.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including:

- GitHub repositories
- Issues and pull requests
- Discussions and project documentation
- Project-related communication channels
- Any public platform where a contributor is representing the Student Pass System project

## Enforcement Guidelines

The following guidelines may be used when determining consequences for violations.

### 1. Correction

**Community Impact:** Minor inappropriate behavior or language.

**Consequence:** A private warning explaining why the behavior was inappropriate and guidance on expected conduct.

### 2. Warning

**Community Impact:** Repeated or more serious violations.

**Consequence:** A formal warning and temporary restrictions on interactions within project spaces.

### 3. Temporary Ban

**Community Impact:** Significant or repeated violations of community standards.

**Consequence:** Temporary suspension from participating in project discussions, issues, pull requests, and related activities.

### 4. Permanent Ban

**Community Impact:** Severe misconduct, harassment, discrimination, threats, or repeated violations after prior enforcement actions.

**Consequence:** Permanent removal from participation in all project spaces.

## Commitment to Students and Educators

Because this project supports student outing management workflows, contributors are expected to maintain a professional, respectful, and educationally appropriate environment.

All interactions should prioritize:

- Student privacy
- Data security
- Respectful communication
- Accessibility and usability
- Constructive collaboration
